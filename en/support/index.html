01

Before reporting an issue

TraderMap requires iOS/iPadOS 16 or later, or tvOS 16 or later, and an internet connection for live data. Market sources can occasionally be delayed or unavailable.

  • Confirm the device is online
  • Close and reopen TraderMap
  • Try another symbol or page
  • Confirm your app and operating system are current
02

What to include

Open a GitHub issue and identify the target: TraderMap (iOS/iPadOS) or TraderMapTV (tvOS). Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, selected language, steps to reproduce and screenshots if useful.

  • Remove personal or confidential information from screenshots
  • Never post passwords, API tokens, seed phrases or private keys
  • Do not post private trading or financial records
03

Contact channel

Technical support, general feedback and feature requests are handled through the repository's public issue tracker. The operator is 17ClassDeveloper (GitHub account: 17classdeveloper-design). Response times are not guaranteed.
